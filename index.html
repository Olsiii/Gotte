About us

Cups that tell your story without costing the earth.

GOTTË is a new business specialized in the production of branded paper cups and paper bags using eco-friendly bio paper and water-based printing technology. We provide modern, high-quality packaging solutions for cafés, restaurants, events, and businesses looking for unique and sustainable branding.

GOTTË continues the long-standing experience of Foto Gagi and Gagi Print, companies with many years of expertise in photo printing, professional printing, and branding services. With a strong commitment to quality, design, and customer satisfaction, our goal is to deliver functional, aesthetic, and environmentally friendly products for both the local and international market.
